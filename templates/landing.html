Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the AI read contracts?

When you upload a supported contract file, the system extracts the text and uses AI to identify key fields like tenant name, rent amount, start and end dates, and adjustment notes, letting you review and confirm before saving.

Does it support different languages and currencies?

ProprietyGuard is designed to process rental contracts across different languages, currencies, and markets, with customizable alert language options.

How are accounts and contract records protected?

Each account is protected with password authentication and only displays contracts belonging to that logged-in user. Always review extracted data and avoid uploading sensitive files until you've verified your data-handling needs.

How do deadline alerts work?

The dashboard tracks lease end dates and flags upcoming expirations, paired with automated email notifications configured for your account.
