Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the AI read contracts?

As soon as you upload a PDF, the system safely extracts the text and the AI engine analyzes the clauses to automatically identify the tenant name, rent amount, start and end dates, and rent adjustment rules.

Does the system work with contracts in any language or currency?

Yes! ProprietyGuard is built for a global workflow, supporting contracts in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, and other languages across any currency.

Are my data and contracts secure?

Yes, each user only accesses their own contracts protected by encrypted password authentication, ensuring complete privacy and confidentiality.

How do deadline alerts work?

The system continuously monitors expiration dates and notifies you in the dashboard (highlighting the critical 30-day window) while also sending an automated email alert.
